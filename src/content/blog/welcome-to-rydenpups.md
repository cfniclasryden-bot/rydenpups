<p class="lead">Hi! We're Luna &amp; Stella — two Dubai dogs (a golden retriever and a cream cocker spaniel) who occasionally let Mom write things on the internet. This is the start of our blog. Here's what to expect.</p>

<div class="quick-answer">
<p><strong>Quick answer:</strong> The rydenpups blog is where we share dog-parent tips, Dubai dog-life updates and behind-the-scenes from brand collabs — written like a friend would, not like a magazine.</p>
</div>

<h2>What we'll write about</h2>

<p>Mostly the stuff our DMs already ask about — where we walk in summer, which treats actually pass the Luna test, what photo gear we use for content, and the small everyday things that make life with two very different dogs work.</p>

<p>If something is sponsored we'll say so up top. If a product made it to the end of a 14-day trial we'll tell you. If Luna refused to eat it on day two, we'll tell you that too.</p>

<h2>How the posts are organised</h2>

<p>Every post has a short summary up top (the bit in the highlighted box above), a few sections of useful info, and an FAQ at the bottom for the questions we get most. If you're skimming on your phone, the summary box should give you the gist in one sentence.</p>

<div class="pull-quote">
We're not trying to be a magazine — we're trying to be the friend you text when your dog refuses to eat.
</div>
